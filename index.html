How to play

1. A team picks a tile. The host reads the survey question, and the first player to buzz answers. Players buzz from their phone, by tapping a team buzzer on screen, or by pressing A or L on a keyboard.
2. If the answer is on the board, tap it to flip it. If it isn't, hit Strike.
3. After 3 strikes, the other team gets one guess to steal the pot.
4. Give the pot to the winning team. The team that lost the round does the forfeit.
5. Mini Games: the team that picked the tile plays the challenge. If they beat it, they win the points. If they don't, they do a forfeit.
6. One tile on the board is a Daily Double, and its pot is worth twice as much.

The survey numbers are made up for fun, not from a real poll.
